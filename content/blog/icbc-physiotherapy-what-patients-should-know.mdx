If you've been in a motor vehicle accident in BC, you may be eligible for treatment support
through ICBC. Many patients aren't sure how the process works or what happens at a first
appointment. Here's a general overview — always confirm the specifics of your own claim with
ICBC directly.

## Do you need a claim number first?

In most cases, you'll want to open your claim with ICBC before booking treatment, since
clinics typically ask for a claim number to bill appropriately. If you haven't opened a claim
yet, that's usually the first step after any needed medical attention.

## What a first physiotherapy visit typically involves

At an initial assessment, a physiotherapist will usually:

- Ask about how the accident happened and how you've felt since
- Assess movement, strength, and areas of pain or restriction
- Discuss a possible plan, which may include hands-on treatment, exercise, and education
- Explain what they can and can't confirm about coverage

Every patient's situation is different, and your practitioner will assess what makes sense
for you rather than following a one-size-fits-all template.

## What to bring

- Your ICBC claim number, if you have one
- A list of current symptoms and how they've changed
- Any relevant medical reports or referral information
- Questions you have about your recovery

## Coverage and what to confirm with ICBC

> Coverage and eligibility for ICBC-funded treatment can vary depending on your claim and
> circumstances. Our team can help you understand the general process, but we can't guarantee
> what will or won't be covered — always confirm the details directly with ICBC.

Because rules and limits can change, it's worth checking in periodically, especially if your
treatment plan extends over a longer period.

## How treatment plans can evolve

Recovery isn't always a straight line. Depending on your progress, a plan might combine
[physiotherapy](/physiotherapy-maple-ridge) with [massage therapy](/massage-therapy-maple-ridge),
[kinesiology and active rehab](/kinesiology-active-rehab-maple-ridge), or other services as
appropriate. Having multiple disciplines coordinated in one place can make it easier to adjust
your plan as you recover.
