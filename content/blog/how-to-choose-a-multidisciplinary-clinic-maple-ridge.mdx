With several clinics to choose from in Maple Ridge, it can be hard to know what actually
matters. Here's a general guide to what a multidisciplinary approach means and what's worth
considering as you compare options.

## What "multidisciplinary" actually means

A multidisciplinary clinic offers multiple types of care — for example,
[physiotherapy](/physiotherapy-maple-ridge), [massage therapy](/massage-therapy-maple-ridge),
[chiropractic care](/chiropractor-maple-ridge), [kinesiology and active rehab](/kinesiology-active-rehab-maple-ridge),
and [acupuncture](/acupuncture-maple-ridge) — under one roof. The idea is that practitioners
can more easily coordinate with each other rather than working in isolation.

## Questions worth asking

When comparing clinics, it can help to ask:

- What disciplines are available, and can they be combined into one plan?
- Do practitioners communicate with each other about shared patients?
- What's the process for ICBC or WSBC claims, if that applies to you?
- Are appointments generally available within a reasonable timeframe?
- Does the clinic offer the specific services you're likely to need, such as
  [orthotics and bracing](/orthotics-bracing-maple-ridge) or [shockwave therapy](/shockwave-therapy-maple-ridge)?

## Why coordinated care can be helpful

When your practitioners can communicate, your plan may adapt more easily as your situation
changes. For example, if physiotherapy identifies that massage or kinesiology could help
support your goals, that referral can often happen quickly within the same clinic.

> A multidisciplinary setup can support more coordinated care, but it doesn't replace
> individual assessment. Each practitioner will still assess your specific situation, and
> outcomes vary from person to person.

## Practical considerations

Beyond the range of services, it's worth thinking about:

- **Location and hours** — does it fit your schedule and commute in Maple Ridge?
- **Insurance and billing** — does the clinic handle direct billing or ICBC claims, and can they explain the general process?
- **Fit with your practitioner** — do you feel comfortable communicating with them?
- **Range of services** — will you likely need more than one discipline over time?

## When a single-service clinic might be enough

Not everyone needs a multidisciplinary approach. If your concern is narrow and well-defined —
for example, a single course of massage for tension — a specialized provider may suit you
just as well. A multidisciplinary setup tends to matter more when your needs are complex or
likely to evolve, such as after an accident or with a chronic condition.
