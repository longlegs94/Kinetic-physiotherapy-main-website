After a motor vehicle accident, early treatment often focuses on pain, mobility, and gentle
movement. As symptoms settle, many recovery plans shift toward active rehab — a more
structured, exercise-based phase focused on rebuilding strength and confidence. Here's a
general look at how that transition tends to work.

## What active rehab means

Active rehab typically involves progressive, supervised exercise aimed at restoring
strength, endurance, and function that may have been affected by the accident. It's usually
guided by a kinesiologist working alongside your broader care team.

## Why it often comes after initial treatment

In the early days and weeks after an accident, treatment often prioritizes managing pain and
restoring basic movement — sometimes through [physiotherapy](/physiotherapy-maple-ridge) or
[massage therapy](/massage-therapy-maple-ridge). As things improve, the focus can shift
toward rebuilding capacity, which is where [kinesiology and active rehab](/kinesiology-active-rehab-maple-ridge)
often becomes relevant.

## What a program might include

- A functional assessment of strength, movement, and any remaining limitations
- A progressive exercise plan tailored to your goals and daily demands
- Gradual reintroduction of activities relevant to work, sport, or daily life
- Ongoing check-ins to adjust the plan as you progress

Your kinesiologist will assess where you're starting from — recovery timelines vary
significantly between individuals, and there's no fixed schedule that applies to everyone.

## How this fits with an ICBC claim

Active rehab is sometimes included as part of a broader ICBC-supported treatment plan,
alongside other services.

## Signs it might be time to consider active rehab

- Pain has settled enough that movement feels more manageable
- You feel deconditioned or hesitant to return to normal activities
- Your physiotherapist or doctor suggests a more structured strengthening phase
- You're preparing to return to work, sport, or regular routines

## Coordinating your care

Because active rehab often overlaps with other treatment, having services like
[physiotherapy](/physiotherapy-maple-ridge), [chiropractic care](/chiropractor-maple-ridge),
and kinesiology coordinated together can make it easier to adjust your plan as you go.
