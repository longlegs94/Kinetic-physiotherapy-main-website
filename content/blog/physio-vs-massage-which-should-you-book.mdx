"Should I book a physio or a massage?" is one of the most common questions we hear. Both
can help you feel and move better — they just approach it differently. Here's a simple way
to think about it.

## What physiotherapy focuses on

Physiotherapy is assessment-driven rehabilitation. A physiotherapist evaluates how you
move, identifies contributing factors, and builds a plan that often combines hands-on
treatment with exercise and education. It tends to be a strong fit for:

- Injuries and post-surgical recovery
- Pain that limits specific movements
- Rebuilding strength, mobility, and function
- A structured plan with progress tracking

## What massage therapy focuses on

Registered massage therapy uses hands-on techniques to address muscle tension, circulation,
soreness, and stress. It can be a great fit for:

- Muscle tightness and tension
- Stress and relaxation
- Recovery from activity or sport
- General aches and comfort, including [pregnancy massage](/pregnancy-massage-maple-ridge)

Explore [massage therapy in Maple Ridge](/massage-therapy-maple-ridge).

## When you might benefit from both

These services aren't competitors — they often work well together. For example, massage may
help ease tension while physiotherapy rebuilds strength and movement. Because our team works
under one roof, your care can be coordinated when it helps.
