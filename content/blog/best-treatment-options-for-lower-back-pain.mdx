Lower back pain is one of the most common reasons people seek treatment — and the good news
is that most cases improve with conservative care. This overview covers common options and
how to think about choosing one. It's general information; a practitioner should assess your
specific situation.

## Start with movement, not just rest

Extended bed rest is generally no longer recommended for most back pain. Gentle movement,
staying active within comfortable limits, and a guided plan tend to support recovery better.

## Conservative treatment options

Depending on your assessment, a plan might include:

- **[Physiotherapy](/physiotherapy-maple-ridge)** — assessment, hands-on treatment, and a tailored exercise program
- **[Kinesiology / active rehab](/kinesiology-active-rehab-maple-ridge)** — progressive strengthening and movement retraining
- **[Massage therapy](/massage-therapy-maple-ridge)** — easing muscle tension that can accompany back pain
- **[Chiropractic](/chiropractor-maple-ridge)** and **[acupuncture](/acupuncture-maple-ridge)** — additional options some people find helpful as part of a plan

## When to seek prompt medical attention

Some symptoms warrant prompt assessment — for example, numbness or weakness in the legs,
loss of bladder or bowel control, unexplained weight loss, or pain after significant trauma.
If you experience these, contact a doctor right away.

## How to choose a starting point

If your pain is limiting specific movements or following an injury, physiotherapy is often a
sensible first step because it starts with an assessment. If tension and soreness are the
main issue, massage may help. Because our disciplines work together, your plan can adapt over
time.
